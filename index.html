Send to Mac

Share photos and files from your phone to your Mac: by cable, or over Wi-Fi. Version 1.0.0.

1. On the Mac

Install phonepull. It runs in the background and receives what you share into Downloads › phonepull.

Download for Mac

This build isn't signed yet: if macOS won't open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

2. On an iPhone

The iPhone app isn't available here yet. On a Mac with Xcode and the signing team, plugging the iPhone in installs it.

Coming soon

2. On an Android phone

Install the Send to Mac app. Or plug the phone into a Mac running phonepull with USB debugging on, and it installs itself.

Download for Android

When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.

3. Send

  1. Tap Share on a photo or file, then Send to Mac.
  2. Pick your Mac from the list.
  3. The first time over Wi-Fi, click Allow on the Mac.
